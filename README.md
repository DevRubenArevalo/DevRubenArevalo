## Selected work

**[Paper Scout](https://github.com/mso-docs/paper-scout)**  
Research companion that investigates claims in papers. Highlight a claim and an agent searches scholarly sources, classifying each result as supporting, contradicting, qualifying, or related. Chrome extension over a FastAPI backend. Two-developer project for AI Tinkerers Atlanta 2026; I worked on the backend.

**Swarm Alignment Hackathon** (November 2026)  
Research sprint on multi-agent swarm behavior — how environments and incentives drive AI agents to cooperate, compete, or drift from their assigned tasks. Co-organized by the [AI Alignment Foundation](https://www.aialignmentfoundation.org/hackathons/swarm-alignment-2026), Apart Research, and AE Studio.

**[From Nothing](https://devrubenarevalo.github.io/from-nothing/)**  
Endless arcade platformer in vanilla JavaScript. Procedurally generated levels, an acid-trail combat system, and a between-run upgrade shop. Playable in the browser — [source](https://github.com/DevRubenArevalo/from-nothing).
